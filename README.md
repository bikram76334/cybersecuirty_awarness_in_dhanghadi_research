# Cybersecurity Awareness and Digital Safety Practices Among Adolescents in Dhangadhi, Nepal

> An academic research project assessing cybersecurity awareness, digital literacy, and online safety practices among adolescents aged 16–20 in Dhangadhi, Nepal.

---

## Overview

As internet and smartphone usage continues to grow, adolescents face increasing cybersecurity threats such as phishing, malware, identity theft, scams, and privacy breaches.

This research evaluates the level of cybersecurity awareness and digital safety practices among adolescents in Dhangadhi, Nepal, identifies knowledge gaps, and provides evidence-based recommendations for improving cybersecurity education.

---

## Objectives

### General Objective

- Assess cybersecurity awareness and digital safety practices among adolescents aged 16–20 in Dhangadhi.

### Specific Objectives

- Measure cybersecurity awareness levels.
- Identify common cyber threats faced by adolescents.
- Examine the relationship between digital literacy and cybersecurity awareness.
- Analyze digital safety practices.
- Recommend strategies for improving cybersecurity education.

---

## Research Methodology

- **Research Design:** Descriptive Survey
- **Study Area:** Dhangadhi, Kailali, Nepal
- **Target Group:** Adolescents (16–20 years)
- **Data Collection:** Physical questionnaires
- **Educational Institutions:** 6 Colleges
- **Responses Collected:** 395
- **Valid Responses:** 372
- **Sampling Method:**
  - Convenience Sampling
  - Purposive Sampling

## Research Highlights

### Internet Usage

- 43.5% use the internet 2–4 hours daily.
- 12.6% use the internet more than 6 hours daily.

### Cybersecurity Awareness

- 91.6% understand the importance of strong passwords.
- 75.7% know public Wi-Fi risks.
- 73.8% understand unknown-link risks.
- 70.1% know common cyber threats.
- Only 53.5% use Two-Factor Authentication (2FA).
- Only 44.3% can confidently identify suspicious messages.

### Digital Safety Practices

- 86.3% use strong passwords.
- 78.7% never share OTPs.
- 74.4% avoid unknown links.
- 73.1% log out from shared devices.
- Only 50.7% regularly update software.
- Only 37.6% use Antivirus or VPN.

### Behavior Gap

Despite high awareness,

- 47.8% still ignore cybersecurity risks.
- Many reuse passwords.
- Many accept Terms & Conditions without reading them.

This demonstrates a significant gap between cybersecurity knowledge and real-world practice.

---

## Recommendations

- Integrate cybersecurity education into school and college curricula.
- Promote Two-Factor Authentication (2FA).
- Conduct cybersecurity awareness workshops.
- Encourage safe password practices.
- Promote antivirus and VPN usage.
- Develop community-level digital literacy campaigns.
- Create localized awareness content using social media.
